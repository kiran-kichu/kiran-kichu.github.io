Things I learned the hard way

  1. The reported fmax is a guess.The critical path moves as the clock tightens. Sweep and see what closes.
  2. Decap doesn't help static IR drop.Capacitors are invisible to DC analysis. The apparent gain came from removed fillers; only straps moved the worst case.
  3. Doubling strap width halved the IR drop.1.6 → 3.2 µm straps: 33.7 → 15.3 mV on PicoRV32, as resistance scaling predicts. 1.6 µm is Sky130's minimum.
  4. Limits should sit between the steps, not on them.A 6.0 µm² cap sat just under Sky130's 6.26 µm² upsize step and blocked good fixes. 7.0 µm² fixed it.
